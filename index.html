Houses we buy

Most of the houses we buy fall into one of these situations.

  • Pre-foreclosure

    You’re behind on the mortgage and the lender has started foreclosure. If the sale date hasn’t passed, there may still be time to sell the house yourself.

  • Probate or inherited

    You’ve inherited a house, maybe along with other family members, and nobody wants to keep it or fix it up. It can still be full of belongings.

  • Vacant

    Nobody lives there, but you’re still paying taxes, insurance and upkeep.

  • Abandoned

    Boarded up, overgrown, or left alone for years.

  • Condemned

    The city has posted it as unsafe to live in.

  • Needs major repairs

    Foundation, roof, wiring, fire or water damage. You don’t fix anything first.
